AP30 custom firmware · design pass 2 · 148×120 drawn, shown 2×

SchalaOS screens

Game Boy Color menus on 8×8 tiles, colored from the Kingdom of Zeal: Schala's violet and gold, the sky kingdom's blues and cloud white, Kajar at dusk and the Ocean Palace. Every pixel is drawn on the real 148×120 grid, so what you see is what fits on the device. Try the switches, then tell me in chat what to keep.

Decided · Nov 7
  • Compact 6px font everywhere. 24 characters per line.
  • Big scrolling titles on Now Playing.
  • No transitions. Every screen cuts in on the next frame and never blocks a button press.
  • Speed first. Sound within 300 ms of pressing play, and every press redrawn within one frame.
  • Boot straight to music. Playable within 3 seconds, resuming the last track. Library changes are found in the background, shown by the small SCAN marker in the status bar.
  • amuletOS, colored from Zeal. Zeal Sky is the default; Schala, Kajar and Ocean Palace are themes.
  • Lavender pops from Schala: status-bar note, scan dots, cursor, playhead and the boot clouds. Zeal Sky shifts per screen: sky blue for Now Playing, palace blue-green and white for Library and tracks, near-white for the block game.
  • Diamond background, arrow cursor, and the pendant boot splash, shown only while audio warms up.
